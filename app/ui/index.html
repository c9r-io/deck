Send –
Prompts are typed into the session at the scheduled moment (works while detached; the app must be running). "After previous" fires once the session has been quiet for 3 minutes — quiet means no output, which may also be a prompt waiting for you. If deck crashes during delivery, the outcome is shown as ambiguous and never auto-retried: acknowledge it as sent, or explicitly retry with possible duplicate risk. "Every…" is a standing rule — it keeps firing on schedule (optionally only inside a daily time window) until you pause or remove it; a window like 20:00–08:00 wraps midnight. A timed prompt starts a new group; "after previous" joins the last group. ☆ on a group header saves that group as a project template.
